About Us

Painting Excellence,
New Zealand–Wide

Megz Paint Limited brings over 20 years of experience in the painting industry to New Zealand. Our team has honed their skills and expertise overseas, bringing a wealth of knowledge and international standards to our local projects. We are fully compliant with New Zealand building codes and regulations, ensuring all our work is completed to the highest standards of quality and safety.

Whether you're looking to refresh your home, revitalise your commercial space, or undertake a large-scale project, Megz Paint Limited is your reliable partner for exceptional interior and exterior painting solutions.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional painting services that enhance the beauty and value of our clients' spaces, while upholding the highest standards of quality, safety, and professionalism.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of painting solutions in New Zealand, renowned for our commitment to customer satisfaction, innovation, and sustainable practices.

Residential Painting

Our residential painting services are tailored to meet the unique needs of your home. We offer both interior and exterior painting, using high-quality paints that provide a long-lasting finish and vibrant colour. Whether you need a single room or your entire house painted, our skilled team ensures a smooth and flawless finish.

Commercial Painting

We understand the importance of maintaining a professional appearance for your business. Our commercial painting services include interior and exterior painting for offices, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. We work around your schedule to minimise disruption to your operations, delivering exceptional results every time.

Interior Painting

Transform your indoor spaces with our expert interior painting services. We pay close attention to detail, ensuring all surfaces are properly prepared and painted to achieve a flawless look. From colour consultation to the final coat, we are dedicated to enhancing the beauty and comfort of your home or business.

Exterior Painting

Protect and beautify the exterior of your property with our comprehensive exterior painting services. We use durable, weather-resistant paints and coatings to withstand the elements and keep your building looking great for years to come.

Roof Painting

Our roof painting services help extend the life of your roof while enhancing its appearance. We use high-quality, weather-resistant paints that protect against UV rays and moisture, providing a durable and attractive finish.

Deck Painting

Our deck painting services are designed to protect and beautify your outdoor living space. We use premium paints and stains that withstand foot traffic, weather, and UV exposure, ensuring your deck looks great and lasts longer.

Fence Painting

Enhance the curb appeal and durability of your fence with our fence painting services. We use high-quality, weather-resistant paints that protect against the elements and provide a fresh, vibrant look.
